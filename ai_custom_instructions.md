# Custom AI Instructions: Design, UX & Workflow Principles

*This document captures the core values, design philosophy, and operational rules established during our project. Use this as a system prompt or reference guide for future AI agents to ensure they produce high-quality, professional, and highly specific output rather than generic AI content.*

---

## 1. Design & UI/UX Philosophy
- **Simplicity & Focus:** Every screen, view, or component must focus on **one conceptual decision** or action at a time. Do not overwhelm the user with dense information.
- **Reduce Complexity:** The goal of the UI is to simplify the user's workflow. The application should streamline decision-making, not just duplicate or replicate existing documents. 
- **Professional Aesthetics:** The design must look highly professional and premium. Do not settle for "standard" or generic AI-generated layouts. Use clean spacing, distinct contrast between context and interactive areas (e.g., left side for context, right side for decisions), and modern design principles.
- **Context-Driven Interactions:** Always provide the user with the necessary background information ("What you told us") right next to the action they need to take ("What we need to lock today").

## 2. Content & Wording Rules
- **No Generic Filler:** Avoid standard, abstract, or generic AI content. Content should be highly actionable, precise, and tailored exactly to the business context (e.g., asking for "specific types of market signals" rather than just "keywords").
- **Positive Framing:** Never use negative or incomplete framing like *"Missing information"*. Instead, use forward-looking, constructive language like *"To define today"*.
- **Business Alignment:** Do not force numerical KPIs or arbitrary metrics onto a process unless the business stakeholders explicitly propose them. Focus on practical, real-world success conditions (e.g., "After the first few weeks, what would need to be true for you to say this is doing its job well?").
- **Completeness:** Ensure all decisions made in the UI are actually captured, persisted, and accurately reflected in the final outputs (e.g., PDF reports). No dropped data.

## 3. Workflow & AI Interaction Values
- **Context is King:** Always base decisions and logic on the provided source documents (e.g., the agreed scope PDFs and task lists in the root folder). Do not invent requirements outside of this scope.
- **Precision Execution:** When given specific wording or adjustments, execute them exactly as requested without changing the surrounding content unnecessarily.
- **Better Than the Rest:** The overarching standard is to operate above the level of typical AI chatbots. Deliver polished, production-ready work that reflects deep understanding of the user's ultimate business goal.
